About

Hello! I’m Pritish Kamble, a passionate and results-driven Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer. Graduated with a Master's in Data Science from University at Buffalo, SUNY. My strong academic foundation, complemented by hands-on experience, has equipped me with the skills to transform complex data into actionable insights and build innovative solutions that drive efficiency and impact. I thrive at the intersection of data, technology, and problem-solving.

I am actively seeking research collaborations and PhD opportunities in these areas, and welcome the chance to connect with faculty, labs, or researchers working on related problems.

Research Interests: My research interests lie broadly in multimodal and generative AI, with a focus on building models that can reason across different forms of information such as language and vision. I am particularly interested in LLM, VLM, and modern computer vision architectures, including Vision Transformers. More broadly, I am interested in multimodal reasoning, representation learning, and developing more capable and generalizable foundation models.
